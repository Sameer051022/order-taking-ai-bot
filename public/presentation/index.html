How it works

The AI is the conversation layer. Your systems stay the source of truth.

The model never sees a giant menu dump and never keeps the cart in its head. It calls tools; the order engine answers. That is why corrections, deals and branch-specific stock work reliably.

Customer→WhatsApp→AI agent (Claude + 21 tools)→Menu · Cart · PricingDeals · PromotionsBranches · Inventory · Delivery→Checkout · Payment→POS / Kitchen→Status updates

Understands, then calls tools

“Make one large” becomes update_cart_item(units=1, add_modifiers=[Large]). The engine splits the line and re-prices. Ambiguity is sent back as a question, not a guess.

Deterministic cart and deals

Deals are products with required choices (“Family Bucket: choose 2 sides, 1 drink”). Missing choices are reported by the backend; the agent asks only for those.

Branch, stock, fee, ETA

Typed area or a shared pin → nearest open branch in range → zone fee and ETA. Sold-out items at that branch are flagged before checkout, with alternatives.

Explicit confirmation, then POS

The order is only created after the customer says yes to an itemised summary. Status changes in the kitchen become WhatsApp updates automatically.

What the product does

Everything a chain needs on day one.

Natural-language orderingMultiple items, quantities and per-item options in one message. English, Urdu and Roman Urdu.
Corrections“Actually 3”, “no, only 2”, “Sprite instead”, “cancel that one” — applied to the right line, every time.
Menu with variants and modifiersSizes, drinks, customisation, allergens-ready structure. Per-branch pricing possible.
Deals and bundlesFamily buckets, kids boxes, share boxes with “choose 2 of…” rules enforced by the backend.
Branch routing and inventoryShared location or typed area, delivery radius, per-branch stock-outs with alternatives.
Delivery fees and ETAsZone-based fees and time estimates, pickup prep times.
Promotions and couponsCodes with minimum order, caps, product/category scope, one-per-customer, expiry. Explained on request.
Upselling rulesConfigurable triggers → suggestion. Offered once, never pushy, acceptance measured.
Order confirmation and statusItemised summary, explicit yes, then automatic updates: confirmed → preparing → ready → on the way → delivered.
Repeat orders“Same as last time, but Sprite” rebuilds and edits a past order.
Human takeoverCustomer asks for a person or the AI gets stuck → staff reply from the dashboard, then hand back.
Dashboard and analyticsLive board, conversations, menu controls, AI revenue, conversion, upsell rate, response time, peak hours.
